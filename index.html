VisionFit TV — AI-Assisted Fitness Companion for Amazon Fire TV

VisionFit TV transforms the living room television from a passive entertainment screen into an interactive, AI-assisted wellness companion. Designed specifically for Amazon Fire TV and a 10-foot viewing distance, the platform enables households to discover guided routines, follow precise biomechanical movement instructions, track physical activity, and receive sports science recommendations using standard TV remote controls.

Core Capabilities and 10-Foot Television User Experience

100% TV Remote Control Navigation

Every single interactive component is engineered for Fire TV D-Pad directional navigation. Pressing Arrow Keys smoothly navigates focus with high-contrast Electric Lime outlines, while Center Select activates items, Space toggles playback, and Escape returns to previous views. The layout complies with Amazon 5% TV title-safe overscan margins.

Evidence-Based AI Sports Science Coach

VisionFit TV features a built-in deterministic sports science knowledge engine that analyzes logged training history, weekly streaks, and post-session difficulty feedback. If a user logs a strenuous session, the coach suggests an active recovery mobility flow. The coach explains proper joint angles, cervical spine alignment, and safe movement mechanics.

Interactive Active Workout Runner

Active sessions display a 96-pixel tabular countdown timer, clear step-by-step form cues, and vector biomechanical movement demonstrations. Web Audio API synthesized acoustic signals provide 3-2-1 beeps and interval chimes without network buffering delays, while speech synthesis announces upcoming exercises.

Accessible Low-Impact Modifications

Joint health and inclusive accessibility are core priorities. Every exercise includes a single-click accessible alternative—such as swapping dynamic air squats for chair box squats or jumping jacks for low-impact lateral step jacks—ensuring users of all mobility levels can maintain physical activity safely.

Custom Session Generator and Workout Blueprint

The custom workout setup wizard allows users to tailor routines by selecting durations from 10 to 30 minutes, defining goals such as fat burn, hypertrophy, or mobility, and selecting available equipment like medium dumbbells or bodyweight only. The recommendation engine dynamically compiles a balanced set of exercises with calculated rest intervals.

All completed workouts, active duration, calories burned, and streaks are persisted in local storage and synchronized with a high-performance SQLite backend.

AI Sports Science Coaching and Biomechanical Diagnostics

The AI coach evaluates historical workouts and user difficulty ratings (Easy, Just Right, Very Tough) to adapt training schedules in real time:

  • Squat Alignment: Cues outward 10–15° foot flare and active knee tracking to prevent patellar valgus collapse.
  • Active Recovery Protocol: Prescribes parasympathetic joint decompression routines when central nervous system fatigue is detected.
  • Progressive Overload: Increments interval duration and density when prior sessions are rated easy.

Physical Activity Tracking and Consistency Streaks

Tracks daily activity across four core disciplines (HIIT, Strength, Mobility, and Core). Visual progress charts render 7-day volume trends toward the World Health Organization’s recommended 150-minute weekly physical activity threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does VisionFit TV work with a standard Fire TV Remote?

VisionFit TV uses a custom spatial navigation engine that captures Arrow Key directional inputs and translates them into smooth focus transitions with high-visibility Electric Lime outlines.

Does VisionFit TV require an external webcam or wearable sensor?

No external sensor is required. The core application provides fully guided interval workouts, acoustic beeps, and vector exercise animations with zero hardware dependencies. An optional optical flow repetition tracker is available when a compatible camera is connected.

How are workout sessions persisted?

All workouts, streaks, and settings are saved locally using high-reliability browser storage and synchronized with an asynchronous SQLite FastAPI backend.
