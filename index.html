VisionFit TV — AI-Assisted Fitness Companion for Amazon Fire TV

Transform your television from a passive entertainment screen into an interactive fitness companion. Designed from the ground up for a 10-foot viewing distance, complete TV remote navigation, and evidence-based sports science coaching without requiring external cameras or wearables.

Key Features & TV Capabilities

100% TV Remote Controllable

Navigate seamlessly with Fire TV D-Pad direction keys, Center Select, and Back navigation. High-contrast Electric Lime focus states and overscan safe margins protect your viewing experience.

AI Sports Science Coach

Analyzes your logged activity and post-workout fatigue feedback to recommend recovery flows, prescribe progressive overload, and explain biomechanical form cues to protect joints.

Interactive Active Workout Runner

Giant 10-foot countdown timers, synthesized 3-2-1 acoustic signals via Web Audio API, vector biomechanical exercise animations, and single-click accessible low-impact alternative exercises.
